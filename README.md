# IMI Elevate

Upgrade the IMI Technologies website into a premium, investor-ready, client-ready African technology company website using the following full specification.

WEBSITE PURPOSE
The website must help IMI Technologies:
1. Get clients for IMI services.
2. Show the IMI portfolio.
3. Build trust for investors and partners.
4. Sell tech products and service packages.
5. Recruit team members.

BRAND
Company name: IMI Technologies
Brand message: Inquire. Motivate. Inspire.
Tone: Premium, executive, serious, trustworthy, futuristic.
Positioning: A serious African technology company offering websites, apps, AI strategy, automation, design, branding, media, and consulting.

TARGET AUDIENCE
Small businesses, student entrepreneurs, churches, schools, NGOs, corporate clients, startups, political organizations, campus organizations, and business owners.

DESIGN STYLE
Use a luxury tech design style with futuristic AI visuals, dark mode, glowing visuals, glassmorphism, premium executive spacing, and strong mobile responsiveness.
Make the website feel investor-ready and client-ready. Avoid generic agency design.

IMI COLOUR PALETTE
- Deep black as the main background.
- Metallic gold for premium accents, borders, icons, highlights, and CTA details.
- Royal blue for gradients, glow effects, hover states, section backgrounds, and technology visuals.
- White and light grey for readable text.
- Avoid random colours outside this palette.

GLOBAL DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
- Dark mode across the full website.
- Glassmorphism cards.
- Glowing gold and royal blue borders.
- Premium spacing.
- Modern typography.
- Luxury executive layout.
- Futuristic AI circuit lines and digital grid patterns.
- Smooth page transitions.
- Strong mobile responsiveness.
- Sticky premium navigation.
- Footer with contact details, social links, WhatsApp, newsletter signup, and quick links.

ANIMATION REQUIREMENTS
- Hero text fades upward.
- Service cards glow on hover.
- Project cards reveal details on hover.
- Portfolio items scale slightly on hover.
- CTA buttons have a subtle blue and gold pulse glow.
- Smooth page transitions.
- Floating WhatsApp button.
- Scroll reveal animations across sections.
- Animated homepage particles.
- AI circuit line motion in the hero.
